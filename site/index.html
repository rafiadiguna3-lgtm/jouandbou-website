Marboy House

jou.house 30

Project Type : Design and Build
Area : 91 / 110 sqm
Concept : Modern tropical house

A modern tropical home arranged around a balance of openness and privacy. Openings bring natural light and fresh air into the house while sheltered spaces offer comfort and seclusion. Connections between indoor rooms and the courtyard keep the home close to its surroundings.

The concept of Rumah Anas about human does not apply only to its name, it is also applied in its concept of form and how we design the house

Inspired by the meaning of Anas—human in Arabic—the architectural form of Rumah Anas is derived from the composition of the human body. The massing is organized into three parts: left, center, and right. The central mass symbolizes the head and torso, serving as both the heart of the home and its architectural focal point. The flanking masses accommodate supporting functions, creating a balanced composition that expresses the project's concept.

Light and privacy

Carefully placed openings bring light and air into the house while protecting quieter, more private rooms. The courtyard keeps everyday spaces connected to nature.

rumah anas

jou.house 01

Project Type : Design and Build
Land : 135 sqm
Building : 120 sqm
Concept : Contemporary House

Our very first residential project, located in Cimanggis, near Cibubur, West Java.

Named Anas, meaning human, the house was envisioned as a place where meaningful interactions naturally unfold, socially, spiritually, and with the surrounding environment. Designed for a young couple with strong family ties, it serves not only as a home but also as a welcoming gathering place where relationships can grow and memories are shared.

The concept of Rumah Anas about human does not apply only to its name, it is also applied in its concept of form and how we design the house.

Inspired by the meaning of Anas—human in Arabic—the architectural form of Rumah Anas is derived from the composition of the human body. The massing is organized into three parts: left, center, and right. The central mass symbolizes the head and torso, serving as both the heart of the home and its architectural focal point. The flanking masses accommodate supporting functions, completing a balanced composition that expresses the project's core concept.

A home, five years on

A revisit to the first Jouhouse shows how the home has grown with its owners. Now known as Rumah Atma, familiar spaces have become more personal through everyday use, planting and gradual changes.

rumasoka

jou.house 10

Project Type : Design and Build
Land : 135 sqm
Building : 150 sqm
Concept : Tropical modern minimalist

Another finished hook type of jouhouse project located in Cibubur is designed in a modern tropical concept of architecture and interior. However, the main idea was to create a more stylish and compact visual of design that translate the client’s needs and wild ideas even in a limited site rather than the common modern tropical concept that is usually represented in a more massive and luxurious type of design.

As maintaining our concern in an efficient, minimalist and healthy design is still our priority while designing the house, open space concept with a lot of openings that builds a fine connection between the indoor and green outdoor area and well-circulated natural air and light came off a great idea that translates all the briefs.

The order of our nature-enthusiastic client is want to have a spacious garden within the house. Moreover, the hook site location, benefiting of gaining more greenery area around the house. The open plan 1st floor sits next to the inner courtyard, which becomes the house's main spectacle, giving the whole area access toward the garden visually and physically.

We keep exploring the whole design to be as simple as possible, be it massing, color tone, opening, or layout to give the highlight to the “SPECIAL ELEMENT”.

The interior is filled with abundant natural light from large openings around the house, supporting healthier living and more efficient electrical usage in daytime. In contrast with the first floor, the second floor is dominated by white tones, aligned with the exterior. Natural stone flooring creates a calm atmosphere for daytime activities and rest at night.

Natural elements and openings establish a fine connection between indoor and outdoor spaces. In the bathrooms, generous skylights frame the clouds and stars, while natural stone walls extend the same material language into the private areas.

Material and garden

On a compact corner site, proportion keeps the house approachable at close range. Brick screens, natural stone and planted edges bring texture to a simple form.

Jangkrik House

jou.house 19

Project Type : Design and Build
Land : 180 sqm
Building : 100 sqm
Concept : Tropical modern minimalist

A modern interpretation of rumah nenek, shaped by the owners’ wish for the warmth of a familiar home. Two building masses follow the site’s axis, leaving room for the house to grow as the family’s needs change.

An east-facing facade is sheltered by a generous gable roof, overhangs and recessed openings. Perforated brickwork draws air through the house. Between the two masses, a semi-outdoor connector opens towards the backyard—an everyday place to pause, gather and enjoy the breeze.

about us

Bismillah,

Jou and Bou Studio is an Architecture and Interior Design Studio that focuses on functionality and problem solving design. We are a proffesional and responsible design studio with organic approach on handling the works, in hope for not only resulting on a sustainable design but also a long-lasting, friendly and bona fide relationship with the clients and users

Jou and Bou comes from word Joy and Boy, We took out Y and change them into U because we believe U are the most important. (pun intended)

We choose Joy because Joy is the end results that we hope for from our clients and also from our team, whether it is in the process of work or after the work is finished.

And Boy because boys will be boys (in positive way), We want to keep our creativity fresh and our mind opened while creating our pieces. keep friendly and buoyant is a must.

Jou and Bou studio strives to be a design studio that elevates people's lives through efficiency, problem solving design and environmental conciousness

our services

architecture . interior . furniture

Jou and Bou Studio provides services in Architecture, Interior and Furniture Design and Build. You can use our services separately, design only or build only or a whole package from design to production/build.

In Design service, you can get free charge of consultation*, concept design which will be developed together, working drawing, 3D visualization and 3D renderings*. All of these services can be requested separately or in a whole package.

In Build service, you can hire us for consultation, build from scratch, renovation and built-in furniture production. All you need to do is share with us your design drawings, 3D file, or just images of reference, then we will translate those drawing and image into working drawings and realize your dream spaces.

scope of works

Insya Allah, our strong will to learn to become an accountable and responsible design studio, also with the help of knowledges and experiences, helps us creating any kind of space you want us to design and build from residential, retail, office, commercial, hospitality, public space, etc.

we divide our works into two categories

Jou is our code to specify our real project. In Jou posts you will see not only concept design, diagram or working drawing, but also existing site, work in progress and some updates and results.

Bou is our code to specify our concept project. In Bou posts you will see concept design only, such as diagram, working drawing, some 3D visualization and animation
